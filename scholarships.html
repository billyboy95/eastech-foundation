Scholarship & Access Fund

Bursaries and wraparound support for talented youth, with priority for women, rural students, and learners entering technical fields.

What the fund covers

  • Tuition and programme fees within the EASTC ecosystem
  • Tools of the trade, PPE, and learning materials
  • Transport and rural access support where needed
  • Mentorship, career guidance, and workplace readiness
  • Priority pathways for women in engineering, ICT, and artisan trades

Eligibility snapshot

South African or African youth with demonstrated academic or technical potential, financial need, and a commitment to complete an EASTC, Artisan College, or EASTECH Institute programme. Selection is competitive and need-sensitive.
